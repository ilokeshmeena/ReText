**ReText** is a user-friendly Android application designed to help you recover and view deleted notifications and messages from your favorite Android apps. Never miss important information again!

## Features

* **Comprehensive Recovery:** ReText diligently monitors and stores incoming notifications and messages, allowing you to retrieve them even after they've been dismissed or deleted from the source app.
* **App-Specific Tracking:** You can easily view deleted notifications and messages categorized by the originating application, making it simple to find what you're looking for.
* **Intuitive Interface:** The app boasts a clean and straightforward user interface for effortless navigation and a seamless recovery experience.
* **Lightweight and Efficient:** ReText is designed to be lightweight and have minimal impact on your device's performance and battery life.
* **Customizable Settings:** Configure which apps you want ReText to monitor, ensuring you only save the notifications and messages that are important to you.

## Installation

1.  Go to the [ReText GitHub repository](https://github.com/ilokeshmeena/ReText).
2.  Download the latest APK file from the [Releases](https://github.com/ilokeshmeena/ReText/releases) section.
3.  Enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android device's settings if you haven't already.
4.  Install the downloaded APK file.
5.  Launch ReText and follow the on-screen instructions to grant the necessary permissions (notification access).

## How to Use

1.  **Grant Notification Access:** Upon first launch, ReText will guide you through the process of granting it access to your device's notifications. This permission is crucial for the app to function correctly.
2.  **Select Apps to Monitor:** Within the app settings, you can choose which applications you want ReText to track for deleted notifications and messages.
3.  **View Recovered Items:** Navigate to the main screen of ReText to see a list of applications with recovered notifications and messages. Tap on an app to view the deleted content.
4.  **Manage Settings:** Customize your experience by adjusting settings such as notification preferences and monitored applications.

## Permissions

ReText requires the following permission to function correctly:

* **Notification Access:** This permission is essential for the app to listen for and store incoming notifications and messages. ReText **does not** store any personal data beyond the content of notifications and messages from the apps you choose to monitor.
